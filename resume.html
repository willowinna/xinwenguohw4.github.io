Optimizing Investment Portfolios Based on LSTM and Random Forest Models  |  06/2024–07/2024

Advisor: Prof. Justin Holmer, Brown University

Independently developed a Python web scraping script to retrieve the daily adjusted closing prices of 15 stocks from 2022 to 2024 from Yahoo Finance and completed data preprocessing.

  • Designed the LSTM model using the build_lstm_model function and built a Random Forest regression model with 100 trees and a random seed of 42.

  • Compared the predictions of LSTM and Random Forest with the Markowitz model and Monte Carlo simulation to obtain optimal portfolios, and tested performance by comparing Sharpe ratios, returns, and risks.

  • Results: the Random Forest portfolio had an average daily return of 0.2% and a standard deviation of 0.87%, while the LSTM portfolio had an average daily return of 0.18% and a standard deviation of 1.08%.

The Research on Factors Influencing the Frequency of Cannabis Use  | 05/2024–07/2024

Advisor: Prof. Patrick Rebeschini, University of Oxford

  • Obtained the original dataset from Kaggle, screened out 1,885 valid samples, extracted 10 core variables, completed data standardization, and conducted descriptive statistical analysis using R.

  • Completed the modeling, performance testing, and visualization of the OLS model, LASSO regression model, Ridge regression model, and Random Forest model with 100 decision trees using R.

  • Conducted a horizontal comparison of the four models: LASSO regression had the smallest Mean Squared Error (MSE) of 2.6916 and was identified as the optimal prediction model.

  • Verified that the probability of cannabis use among people aged 25–34 was 68.1% higher than among people aged 65 and above; for each 1-point increase in Oscore, the risk of cannabis use frequency increased by 0.711 levels.

  • Proposed intervention suggestions: designed preventive education targeting adolescent groups with high Oscore, and used nicotine use history as an indicator for screening the risk of cannabis abuse.
